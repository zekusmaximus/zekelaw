Introduction
When high-stakes regulatory scrutiny and legislative challenges threaten your organization’s objectives, you need more than a traditional lobbyist—you need a seasoned legal strategist.

The Law Offices of Jeffrey “Zeke” Zyjeski offers independent, elite government relations and administrative law counsel. After over two decades as a key figure at one of Connecticut’s most prominent legacy firms, Zeke Zyjeski brings the institutional knowledge of a Capitol heavyweight combined with the un-conflicted, focused attention of a solo practitioner.

[About Me]
Headline: Decades of Capitol Experience. A Foundation in the Law.
My career at the Connecticut Capitol began in 1998 as a staffer for the Legislature’s Judiciary Committee. That foundational experience instilled a deep, procedural understanding of how laws are drafted, debated, and enacted in Hartford.

For over two decades, I served as a key figure at Gaffney, Bennett & Associates and the Law Offices of Jay F. Malcynsky, P.C., representing a vast roster of corporate and institutional clients. Now, as an independent practitioner, I offer clients a distinct advantage: the strategic depth of a large firm veteran, delivered with the agility and direct access of a solo practice.

I am not just a government relations professional; I am an attorney admitted to practice before the United States Supreme Court. My commitment to the legal profession extends to my leadership roles, having served proudly as Secretary of the Connecticut Bar Association and as a Board member of Connecticut Lawyers Concerned for Lawyers.

This unique combination of legislative mastery and elite legal credentials ensures that my clients receive advocacy that is not only politically astute but legally unassailable.
